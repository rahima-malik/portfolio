About Me

Hi, I’m Rahima Malik, a Data Science student at Pak-Austria Fachhochschule (PAF-IAST). I work with Python and C++, and I enjoy taking messy, confusing data and turning it into something clear and useful. For me, good work starts with asking the right question and letting the data answer it.

I’ve built a WhatsApp message scheduler in Python, plus a patient triage system and an ATM simulation in C++ using object-oriented programming. I’m also certified in data literacy through IBM SkillsBuild.

Outside the classroom, I believe leadership is about service as much as skill. I’m looking for internships and projects where I can learn, contribute and grow in data science.
